### Order Total Discrepancies – Detailed Results

The investigation identified 100 orders with differences between recorded order totals and calculated item totals.

The discrepancies are divided into two groups:

- **67 orders (67%):** Contain order items, but their calculated item totals differ from the recorded order totals.
- **33 orders (33%):** Have no associated order-item records.

**Business decision:**

The original data will remain unchanged. Both recorded order totals and calculated item revenues will be preserved as separate metrics. Orders without item details will be excluded from product-level revenue analysis.

The reasons behind the 67 remaining discrepancies cannot be established from the available data and require further investigation.
